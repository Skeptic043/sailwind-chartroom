Field guide

Chartroom shortcuts

VSelect marks and pan the chart
LAdd a location
RPlot route waypoints
MMeasure a temporary bearing and distance
ARough area: press where it is centred and drag out the radius. Drag the edge of a selected area to resize it.
SSearch area: press on the port or position that gave the direction, drag toward it and let go at the distance. The direction snaps to compass points.
SpaceHold while dragging to pan from any tool
CtrlHold to place freely. Otherwise drawing tools pull gently onto nearby marks, lines and crossings, shown by a ring.
ShiftAdd or remove items from the selection. Shift-drag empty water to select an area.
IOpen the island library. A location named for an island displays its outline automatically.
BoatSelect a location or search area, then choose a boat for its cyan lower-right icon. Search-area boats carry into the resolved location.
WAdd a regional wind indicator: drag in the direction the wind is blowing toward.
DeleteRemove the selected chart items
Ctrl ZUndo the last chart edit
FFit locations, route and track in view

Finding an island: when someone gives you a direction and distance, draw a search area from where you heard it. Areas with the same name brighten only where they all overlap. Once you find the island, choose Resolve to location and click where it is.

Routes: Save route keeps a finished route on the chart and starts a new one. Saved routes are listed under Chart items. Click a saved route's line to select or drag the whole route, or click one of its waypoints to edit that point.

Distance model: the original Sailwind map uses a flat chart and approximately 140 nautical miles per degree. Chartroom preserves that convention; it does not treat the game world as a globe.

Long plots: while a measurement, search or area is active, hold the pointer against the chart edge briefly to pan in that direction.

Your charts: Chartroom saves automatically and keeps a copy from your previous launch. Use File, Back up all charts to keep your own copy, or to move your charts to another computer.

Layout: use the two panel buttons at the top to hide either sidebar. Drag a sidebar's inner edge to resize it.

About

Sailwind Chartroom

A fan-made charting desk for Sailwind. It is not made by or affiliated with the developers of Sailwind.

Your charts stay on this computer unless you export them. Chartroom only goes online to check GitHub for updates, which you can turn off in File, Settings.
