Turn the route into a race anyone can enter. Waypoints on islands become those islands, while every other waypoint becomes a mark 3 miles wide. The first waypoint is the start, and a route that returns there also finishes there.

A race can't be changed once runs have been entered. GitHub opens with the race filled in. Submit the form there to publish it.

Field guide

Chartroom shortcuts

VSelect marks and pan the chart
LAdd a location
RPlot route waypoints
MMeasure: drag between two points, or click each end, for the distance, bearing and passage time. The line stays until you delete it or measure again. Keep as a chart line leaves it on the chart in red, blue or green.
DDead reckoning: plot your position from your compass course, chip log speed and hours sailed.
ARough area: press where it is centred and drag out the radius. Drag the edge of a selected area to resize it.
SSearch area: press on the port or position that gave the direction, drag toward it and let go at the distance. The direction snaps to compass points.
SpaceHold while dragging to pan from any tool
CtrlHold to place freely. Otherwise drawing tools pull gently onto nearby marks, lines and crossings, shown by a ring.
ShiftAdd or remove items from the selection. Shift-drag empty water to select an area.
IOpen the island library. A location named for an island displays its outline automatically.
BoatSelect a location or search area, then choose boats for its cyan icons beside it. Search-area boats carry into the resolved location.
WAdd a regional wind indicator: drag in the direction the wind is blowing toward.
DeleteRemove the selected chart items
LockThe padlock beside a selected item, or Lock selected in the Edit menu, keeps it in place until you unlock it.
Ctrl ZUndo the last chart edit
FFit locations, route and track in view

Finding an island: when someone gives you a direction and distance, draw a search area from where you heard it. Areas with the same name brighten only where they all overlap. Once you find the island, choose Resolve to location and click where it is.

Routes: Save route keeps a finished route on the chart and starts a new one. Saved routes are listed under Chart items. Click a saved route's line to select or drag the whole route, or click one of its waypoints to edit that point. Add waypoints continues a saved route from its last waypoint.

Distance model: the original Sailwind map uses a flat chart and approximately 140 nautical miles per degree. Chartroom preserves that convention; it does not treat the game world as a globe.

Long plots: while a measurement, search or area is active, hold the pointer against the chart edge briefly to pan in that direction.

Without a recording: Dead reckoning carries your position forward from your last known position, using your compass course, chip log speed and hours sailed. Plot a new DR position whenever you change course or speed. Each circle shows how far that DR position may be off.

Following your boat: press J in game to start recording with Voyage Recorder, then choose File, Recorded track, Follow live. Chartroom finds the recording by itself. When it finds none, it asks for the file instead, such as the coords file in Coordinate Viewer's plugin folder. Each position recorded joins the chart you started on.

Scrambled Seas: turn on ExternalSave in Scrambled Seas' settings and save your game, then use File, Import to open the scramble_<slot>.xml file in its plugin folder. Chartroom makes a new chart with the Scrambled Seas map in View, showing where that world's islands are.

Your charts: Chartroom saves automatically and keeps a copy from your previous launch. Use File, Back up all charts to keep your own copy, or to move your charts to another computer.

Layout: use the two panel buttons at the top to hide either sidebar. Drag a sidebar's inner edge to resize it.

About

Sailwind Chartroom

A fan-made charting desk for Sailwind. It is not made by or affiliated with the developers of Sailwind.

Your charts stay on this computer unless you export them. Chartroom only goes online to check GitHub for updates, which you can turn off in File, Settings.
