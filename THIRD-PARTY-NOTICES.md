## Sailwind theme paper and scroll

The files in `assets/sailwind` were cropped, resized, rotated and darkened from these images, both
released under the [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) public
domain dedication. `scripts/prepare-sailwind-art.ps1` makes them from the original downloads.

- Paper: [old paper](https://opengameart.org/content/old-paper) by Flixberry Entertainment
  (`old_paper_paper_1_1_800x500.png`), cropped and cross-faded into a seamless tile.
- Tool button scrolls: [Scroll UI Container](https://opengameart.org/content/scroll-ui-container) by
  Chloe Wolfe, Cairo Creative Studios (`container.png`).
